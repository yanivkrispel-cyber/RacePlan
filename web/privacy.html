Privacy Policy

RacePlan by Coach Krispel · Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what information RacePlan (“we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. RacePlan is operated by Yaniv Krispel, trading as CoachKrispel, Israel.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, used to sign in and identify your account.
  • Race planning data: race courses you upload (GPX files), target times, pace plans, and related settings such as weather or heat adjustments.
  • Garmin connection data: if you choose to connect your Garmin Connect account, we store the access credentials (tokens) that Garmin issues so we can send workouts on your behalf. We never see or store your Garmin password.
  • Technical data: basic logs (such as error reports and timestamps) needed to operate and secure the service.

2. Garmin Connect integration

Connecting Garmin is optional and happens only after you explicitly authorize it through Garmin’s own consent screen. Once connected:

  • We use the Garmin Training API only to send a structured workout created from your race plan to your Garmin Connect account, and only when you choose to do so.
  • We do not read, collect, or store your Garmin activity, health, or location data.
  • You can disconnect at any time from within RacePlan or from your Garmin Connect account settings. When you disconnect, we delete the stored Garmin tokens.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, save, and display your race plans.
  • To deliver your plans to Garmin Connect when you request it.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the service, and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where your data is stored

RacePlan runs on Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Your data is stored and processed by Google as our hosting provider, under Google’s security and data-protection terms. Data is encrypted in transit.

5. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Garmin, when you choose to send a workout to your Garmin Connect account;
  • Google (Firebase), as our hosting and infrastructure provider;
  • Authorities, if required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual race plans at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and all associated data by emailing us. We will complete deletion within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children

RacePlan is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and notify users in the app.
